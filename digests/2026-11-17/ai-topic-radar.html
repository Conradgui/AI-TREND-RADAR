Locational Marginal Pricing of Compute: Co-optimizing Compute and Energy for Transparent Data Center Flexibility
ArXiv · 模型与技术突破
55 观察

Demand from data centers and AI compute is growing rapidly and is increasing stress on the power grid. This load could be flexible through user adjustments and provide relief during energy scarcity events, yet today its flexibility is provided through utility call-ups or system-operator load shedding, and is invisible to the users whose workloads are being curtailed. In this work, we propose to quantify and couple the prices of compute and energy in a single optimization problem. The resulting compute price reflects the marginal cost of the binding constraints and decomposes into components indicating which grid, power, hardware, or service requirement causes a price surge. The economic interpretation updates the price with real-time conditions and gives users a signal for potential flexibility provision. The co-optimization ultimately connects compute and energy markets while providing a signal for relief of stress on both the grid and the compute resources.
